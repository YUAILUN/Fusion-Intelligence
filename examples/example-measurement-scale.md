# Fusion Report — Chinese adaptation and validation of an AI-literacy scale for healthcare staff
**Mode:** standard · **Panel:** 8 lenses, 5 families

## Bottom line
The plan is publishable only if **content validity and structural validity are treated as separate, ordered
gates**, and if "AI literacy" is bounded before translation. The biggest risk is not translation quality; it is
a construct that may be defined for a different population and era than your sample.
**Confidence:** Moderate — the logic is well established, but nothing here was checked against the specific
source instrument or the target-population data.

## 1. Question brief & assumptions
- Question: is the adaptation-and-validation plan sufficient to support claims of validity in Chinese healthcare staff?
- Decision fed: go/no-go on study design before ethics submission.
- Assumptions: A1 the source scale was developed for a general or non-clinical population · A2 target users are
  physicians, nurses and allied staff across hospital levels · A3 a single online survey is planned.

## 4. Where it splits
| # | Claim | Camp A | Camp B | Crux | Resolves with |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Keep the source scale's dimensions | psychometrics: preserves comparability | language/practice: dimensions may not carry across | **Definitional (D) + Scope (S)** | cognitive interviews + pre-registered rule for when to modify structure |
| S2 | Report a total score | measurement: only if a general factor is supported | practice: users want one number | **Values (V)** | decide intended use; report subscales unless total is supported |

## 5. Blind spots & missing evidence
- G1 No data on how the source instrument was developed (population, sample, structure) — retrieve.
- G2 Measurement invariance across professions is not planned.
- G3 No plan for response-style effects in a self-report online survey.

## 6. Recommended path
**No-regret moves**
1. Run cognitive interviews with 10–15 target users *before* the main survey; record item-level problems.
2. Pre-specify structure-modification rules and the invariance groups.
3. Plan separate samples for EFA and CFA, justified against item count.

**Conditional moves**
- If cognitive interviews show systematic item misunderstanding → revise or add local items, then re-run content-validity review.
- If a general factor is not supported → report subscale scores only.

## 7. What would change this conclusion
- Evidence that the source scale already shows stable structure across professions and languages.
- Interview data showing comprehension is uniformly high.
